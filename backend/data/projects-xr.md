# VR, AR and games projects

Virtual and augmented reality work, largely between 2017 and 2020, built with Unity3D and C#.

Most of the client work here was delivered **freelance through vreestory**, alongside Ljuben's
full-time mobile roles.

## Hood VR — environmental education for schools (Romanian Ministry of National Education, May 2019)

Delivered freelance through **vreestory**.

A virtual reality game teaching children about recycling, built as part of a larger Romanian
government programme to improve knowledge of recyclable items in schools.

It was **presented in more than 20 schools**. Children played against the clock, collecting
different trash items in a virtual forest environment.

The largest-reach project on this list by number of people who actually used it, and one with a
genuine educational objective rather than a commercial one.

## Wincanton Fire Safety (Wincanton plc., September 2019)

Delivered freelance through **vreestory**.

Virtual reality development for Wincanton, a UK logistics company, on fire safety.

VR training for safety-critical procedures — the category of VR application with the clearest
justification, since it lets people rehearse dangerous situations without danger.

## OMV VR — promotional VR game (OMV Romania, September 2018)

Delivered freelance through **vreestory**.

A short VR game advertising a specific brand of fuel. Never published to any app store: the client
chose to deliver it only at promotional events, and the advertising team toured the country with
GearVR headsets, letting people at each location play and rewarding the best player.

Gameplay involved selecting the correct floating 3D molecules with the joystick and dragging them
into position against a time limit.

An unusual distribution model — software built to be experienced in person at events rather than
downloaded.

## Hellboy VR — film promotion (Lions Gate Entertainment Inc., December 2017)

Built during Ljuben's short contract at **SolvedOut**.

A 360-degree video game built with Unity3D as a Google Cardboard promotional experience for the
film *Hellboy: Rise of the Blood Queen* (2019).

## Madara Rider AR — proof of concept at Allterco (June 2017)

Built at **Allterco** as a proof of concept for a potential client project, rather than as
delivered work.

An augmented reality experience promoting Bulgaria as a tourist destination: the application plays
a promotional video when the camera recognises the Madara Rider rock relief. Built with the
**Vuforia SDK** on Unity3D, compiled for Android but portable to iOS.

Proof-of-concept work is worth listing honestly — it demonstrates the ability to evaluate an
unfamiliar technology quickly enough for a business to decide whether to pursue it.

## Personal training projects (2017)

Built in Ljuben's own time while he was moving from pure Android development into real-time 3D.
These are self-directed learning projects, not client work, and are listed as such.

- **Christmas Patterns VR** (November 2017) — a block-building game for children set against a
  360-degree panorama, developed for **Udacity Teamworks #3**. On Google Play as
  `com.abraxas.christmaspatternsvr`.
- **Maze VR** (April 2017) — a Google Cardboard game with a large coin-filled level where the
  player must find a key to unlock the exit. Optimised for Cardboard v1.
- **Puzzler VR** (May 2017) — observe a pattern and repeat it to complete the level. "Simon says"
  in immersive VR.
- **Super Mario Replica** (August 2017) — a replica of Super Mario Bros built with **libGDX**,
  portable to Android, iOS and desktop. First level only, as a proof of concept.

Teaching himself a new platform in his own time is a pattern that repeats: the same instinct
produced this AI portfolio site nearly a decade later.
