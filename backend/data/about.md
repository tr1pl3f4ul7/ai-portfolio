# About Ljuben Vassilev

## Who he is

Ljuben Vassilev is a mobile and software engineer based in Brisbane, Queensland, Australia. He
began his software career in 2016 and has ten years of professional experience.

Mobile development is his core discipline and where most of that decade was spent — Android in
Java and Kotlin, then Flutter and Dart. Around it he has built two further specialisations:
real-time 3D for VR and AR, and application security.

He currently works as a Software Engineer at AI Talent, and previously spent four years at
PropertyMe, where he held two concurrent roles: Software Engineer and Application Security
Engineer.

## How he describes himself

His LinkedIn headline reads: "Software Engineer & AppSec Specialist | Building Secure Code from
the Ground Up | Frameworks don't matter, satisfied customers do!"

That last phrase is a deliberate position. He treats frameworks and languages as interchangeable
tools chosen to fit a problem, and judges work by whether it solved something for the people using
it. His career supports the claim: he has shipped production software in Java, Kotlin, Dart, C#,
JavaScript and Unity/C#, across Android phones, Flutter, VR headsets, industrial barcode scanners
and TV set-top boxes.

## The shape of his career

**Mobile is the spine of it, from 2016 to today.** He started as a junior Android developer at
Allterco in Sofia after completing the IT Talents Training Camp bootcamp, then worked across
consultancy and product companies in banking, telecoms, logistics and retail, before moving to
Australia and to Flutter.

Two specialisations grew alongside it:

- **VR and AR, 2017–2020.** Unity3D work — advertising experiences, educational games commissioned
  by government programmes, and film promotion — largely freelance and alongside full-time mobile
  roles.
- **Security, from 2021.** A Master of Information Technology in Cyber Security and Network
  Security at Charles Sturt University, taken while working full time, which led to an Application
  Security Engineer role at PropertyMe running penetration testing, code review and security
  automation.

The through-line is constrained environments. TV launchers and VR headsets impose hard performance
limits. Banking and property management impose regulatory and audit requirements. Warehouse
scanners impose the constraint that if the software stops, physical work stops.
